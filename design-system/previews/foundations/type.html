Helm is an AI team member that listens where strategy happens, turns signals into outcome charters, and nudges teams when strategic drift begins.

NOTE — the display scale is expressed with arbitrary values (md:text-[22px], md:leading-[0.95], max-w-[860px], max-w-[760px]) rather than scale steps. It is the only place in Helm where type is treated typographically — tracking, leading, and weight contrast all get deliberate handling.
