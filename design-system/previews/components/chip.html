OPEN QUESTION — this is the central design problem in the handoff. Helm needs to distinguish anchored / replaced / additive states and flag drift severity, while "no bright status colors" and "no gamified scoring" rule out the conventional answers (red-amber-green, severity dots, score meters). Weight, border, icon, typography, and position are all still available. Colour is mostly not.
