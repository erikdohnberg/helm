Confirmations and standing conditions. Both state facts; neither reassures.

```jsx
<ToastViewport>
  <Toast message="Attached to Co-sell motion. Nothing entered the quarter." />
  <Toast tone="error" message="Not recorded — the connection dropped. Your text is still here." />
</ToastViewport>

<Alert title="This quarter is locked">FY26 Q1 closed on 31 Mar. New outcomes will enter FY26 Q2.</Alert>
<Alert tone="destructive" title="Retiring removes this outcome from the record">
  Nothing will stand in its place. Replacing keeps it in the record.
</Alert>
```

Toasts are e2 (transient); alerts are flat, because a standing condition is part of the record. No exclamation marks, no "Success!", no emoji.
